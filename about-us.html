Meet the Founder

Rachel Brian

Rachel Brian is the founder and principal animator of Blue Seat Studios. She's a former educator who taught biology, physiology, and math at both the high school and college level, with a background in biology and educational psychology.

She started Blue Seat Studios to make complex topics clearer, more human, and easier to engage with. The goal is simple: create training people will actually pay attention to — using humor, strong storytelling, and thoughtful design.

Rachel is also the author of Consent (For Kids!) (Kirkus starred), The Worry (Less) Book, (Be Smart About) Screen Time, and ADHD!, as well as the forthcoming Bounce-Back Book, all published by Hachette / Little, Brown for Young Readers.

Blue Seat Studios works with a team of experienced animators, illustrators, and voice talent to create content that's thoughtful, well-crafted, and built to connect.
